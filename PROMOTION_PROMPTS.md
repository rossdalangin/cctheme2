## 💡 Usage Tips
- **Consistency:** Use the hex code `#6366F1` (Indigo) and `#020203` (Onyx) in your prompt descriptions to maintain brand alignment.
- **Keywords:** Always include "High-Fidelity", "Elite", "Surgical Precision", and "Minimalist" to get the CloseClient aesthetic.
- **Negative Prompts:** Avoid "cluttered", "cheap", "generic", "cartoonish", and "colorful".
